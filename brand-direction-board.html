Brand direction — three options

A new visual identity for H&A Healthcare Consulting

Three distinct directions, each built from a different part of what H&A actually does — clinical/EHR precision, the referral network the business runs on, and the revenue-cycle billing operation. Same brief throughout: enterprise-credible, benchmarked against eClinicalWorks.com's category, executed at a sharper level than that benchmark itself.

Pick one to carry into the full rebuild — or tell me what to recombine.

DIRECTION 01

Clinical Authority

Grounded in the EHR-optimization side of the business — the visual language of a regulatory document or clinical audit log. A federal-service sans (built for exactly this kind of trust-critical reading) paired with an editorial serif for headlines, and a monospace face reserved for numbers — claims, uptime, dates — since this is a business whose credibility is measured in figures.

DIRECTION 03

Enterprise Ledger

Grounded in the 150-person billing operation — the actual growth engine. Borrows the trust language of modern fintech rather than generic healthcare teal: obsidian ground, a controlled violet no EHR competitor owns (Epic is green, Cerner and eCW are blue), and a monospace face for dollar figures and claim counts. The most "exotic" of the three, closest to the original brief's language.
