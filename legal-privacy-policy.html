Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

H&A Healthcare Consulting ("H&A," "we," "us," or "our") operates hahealthconsulting.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Site collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers the Site itself — how we treat protected health information (PHI) in the course of a client engagement is addressed separately below and is governed by the terms of that engagement, not by this policy.

Information You Provide to Us

The only information the Site actively collects is what you choose to submit through our consultation request form: your name, email address, phone number, the area of the business you're interested in, and any optional message. When you submit that form, it's sent directly by email to our team — it is not stored in a database on this website or shared with any third party for their own use.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and security providers automatically log standard technical information for every visit — such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamps — for security and operational purposes (for example, detecting abuse or diagnosing outages). We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on this Site, and we do not use this information to build a profile of you.

Cookies & Local Storage

The Site uses your browser's session storage — not a persistent cookie — to remember two things for the length of your visit: whether you've already seen the entry animation, and whether the "Get Your Free Consultation" popup has already been shown to you. This information clears automatically when you close your browser and is never sent to us.

The Site is served through Cloudflare, a security and content-delivery provider, which may set its own strictly necessary cookies to protect the Site from abuse (for example, distinguishing real visitors from automated bots). Google Fonts is used for the typefaces on this page, which involves your browser requesting font files directly from Google's servers.

How We Use Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, schedule a consultation, and communicate with current and prospective clients about our services. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Who We Share Information With

We share submitted information only where necessary to operate the Site and respond to you — for example, with our email and hosting providers, who process it on our behalf solely to deliver your message to our team. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain submitted inquiries for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and maintain our business records, and delete or archive them when no longer needed for those purposes.

Protected Health Information (PHI)

This website does not collect or store protected health information through its consultation form. Any PHI shared with H&A in the course of an actual client engagement — for example, during EHR implementation or medical billing work — is governed separately by the terms of that engagement, applicable Business Associate Agreements, and HIPAA, not by this website's Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

You can decline to submit the consultation form — the Site remains fully browsable without it. To request a copy, correction, or deletion of information you've previously sent us, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for healthcare practice administrators and professionals and is not directed at, or knowingly collecting information from, children under 13.

Security

The Site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and sits behind Cloudflare's security network. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect the information submitted to us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Site or our practices. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your information, can be sent to info@hahealthconsulting.com or (832) 800-4352.
